# Open Masjid Display

A prayer display for mosques, schools, and other shared spaces.

## Language

**Display**: The information people see on a monitor or television, including prayer times and the sun visualization.

**Sun visualization**: A view of the sun's position through the day for the selected location, with prayer markers. Its precise geometry and treatment of night remain design decisions.

**Notice**: A short text message chosen by the person configuring the display.
