# Open Masjid Display

A prayer display for mosques, schools, and other shared spaces.

## Language

**Display**: The information people see on a monitor or television, including prayer times and the sun visualization.

**Prayer start time**: The time a prayer's time period begins. This is distinct from the mosque's congregation time.

**Iqamah time**: The mosque-configured time the imam starts the congregational prayer. Each prayer can use an offset from its prayer start time or a fixed clock time. Displays without a congregation can omit iqamah.

**Prayer schedule**: The daily list of prayer start times, with a separate iqamah column when congregation times are enabled.

**Jumu'ah service**: An optional Friday congregation with a configured time labelled "Khutbah starts" and an optional associated iqamah time. A mosque may list one or several services, including three or more; without configured services, the display uses ordinary Dhuhr.

**Sun visualization**: A view of the sun's current position for the selected location that gives a sense of dawn, midday, sunset, and the passage of the day, with prayer markers. Its precise geometry and treatment of night remain design decisions.

**Notice**: A short text message chosen by the person configuring the display.

**Moon illumination**: The percentage of the Moon's apparent disc illuminated as viewed from Earth. This is astronomical information, separate from the selected Hijri calendar.

**White Days**: The 13th, 14th and 15th of the selected Hijri month, as specified in [Jami at-Tirmidhi 761](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:761). A White Days date label is distinct from eligibility for a fasting reminder.
